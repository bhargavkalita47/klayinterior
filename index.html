Meet Kalpita.

Kalpita Bhadra's passion for interiors began in school. In 2017, she began working at Impeccable Imagination in Mumbai, where she built her practice over four years. She returned to her hometown of Guwahati and founded KLAY Interior Studio in 2021.

From smaller rooms to large homes and commercial spaces, the studio approaches every assignment with a belief in listening first, understanding how the space will be used and making it both thoughtful and practical.

“Style is a very personal notion.” That principle continues to guide KLAY's work across homes, offices and showrooms.

OUR APPROACHTHOUGHTFUL FROM START TO FINISH
01

Listen first.

Understanding your needs, routines and aspirations before a design direction takes shape.

Creating spaces with an identity of their own, not simply following a passing trend.
