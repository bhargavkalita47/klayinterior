Meet Kalpita.

Kalpita's interest in interiors began in class nine. In 2017, she joined Ambika Hinduja's Impeccable Imagination in Mumbai. After four years in the practice, she returned to Guwahati and established KLAY Interior Studio in 2021.

Her practice spans residential and commercial interiors, from compact 240-square-foot spaces to projects as large as 8,000 square feet. Each brings a different set of practical requirements, and the studio starts by listening to the client.

The portfolio ranges from three-bedroom homes in Kahilipara to a lighting showroom in Boragaon. Space planning and the way clients use their rooms are central to the studio's work.

OUR APPROACHTHOUGHTFUL FROM START TO FINISH

Listen first.

Protech Prime

A softer palette for a compact home

Project
Residential · 3 BHK
Area
900 sq ft
Interior design
KLAY Interior Studio

In this three-bedroom apartment, KLAY works with pale neutrals and pastels to establish a calm, cohesive interior across 900 square feet. The rooms have distinct functions without departing from that shared palette.

A room for quiet

The bedroom pairs a gentle colour palette with a distinct circular mirror. The guest room includes a white-and-gold temple, bringing a personal element into the home.

Space to read and work

A dedicated study includes a book nook, giving reading and everyday work a place of their own within the apartment.

Digital Empire

An interior built around the experience of light

Project
Commercial · Electronics & lighting showroom
Area
2,200 sq ft
Interior design
KLAY Interior Studio

A lighting showroom serves a different purpose from an ordinary retail floor: visitors need to see fixtures and consider how they might work in their own spaces. At Digital Empire, display areas sit alongside a lounge for conversations with the store's lighting specialists.

Light on display

Chandeliers, pendants and fixtures for indoor and outdoor spaces form the centre of the showroom. The displays bring different lighting options together for customers to explore.

A place for conversation

The lounge gives visitors somewhere to pause, discuss their requirements and make decisions alongside the showroom team.

Hatigaon Duplex

A home arranged around everyday rituals

Project
Residential · Duplex
Area
2,200 sq ft
Interior design
KLAY Interior Studio

The 2,200-square-foot duplex brings together spaces for shared meals, rest, dressing and reading. The project is best understood through these different parts of domestic life rather than as a single decorative scheme.

From shared spaces to private rooms

The dining area establishes the shared side of the home; the master bedroom and dressing area form a more private sequence.

A space of one's own

A dedicated reading corner is also part of the project, alongside the larger rooms that make up the duplex.

Kahilipara, Guwahati

Shubham Heights

Different rooms, different personalities

Project
Residential · 3 BHK
Area
1,500 sq ft
Interior design
KLAY Interior Studio

This three-bedroom home is organized around its living and dining areas, entrance and individual bedrooms. The portfolio reveals how the treatment of each room changes with its purpose.

Coming home

The entrance and living area provide the first impression of the apartment. The entrance introduces timber panelling, while the living room uses upholstered furniture and a patterned wall.

Rooms with their own character

The bedroom and children's room have distinctly different finishes. The children's room introduces colourful storage, while darker timber and metallic tones characterize the other bedroom.
